# NumPy Documentation Team Charter

## Purpose
The NumPy Documentation Team stewards the NumPy official documentation, keeping it 
accurate, comprehensive, and accessible for users and contributors at all 
experience levels. The team also cultivates a contributor community 
through continuous support and mentorship.

## Responsibilities

The Documentation Team is responsible for:
* Maintaining and improving all NumPy official documentation.
* Coordinating and reviewing documentation contributions.
* Maintaining documentation standards, style guides, and best practices.
* Collaborating with other NumPy teams to ensure documentation accurately reflects project
  developments.
* Identifying documentation gaps and prioritizing improvements.
* Onboarding and supporting documentation contributors.


## Team Leadership

### Documentation Team Lead

The Documentation Team shall be led by a Documentation Team Lead.

The Lead is responsible for:

* Facilitating and leading discussions related to the NumPy official documentation 
across the project. 
* Helping establish priorities and long-term direction for the project documentation.
* Coordinating and supporting the work of the Documentation Team.
* Onboarding and supporting documentation contributors.
* Ensuring continuity of documentation initiatives, team operations, and leadership
  transitions.

The Documentation Team Lead shall serve a term of one year.

There is no limit on the number of terms an individual may serve.

At the conclusion of the term, the Deputy Lead shall ordinarily assume the role, subject 
to confirmation by the Steering Council. The introduction of a 1-year term is intended to 
strengthen the stewardship of NumPy documentation, distribute leadership responsibilities, 
and create opportunities for leadership development within the NumPy contributor community.

### Deputy Lead

The Documentation Team shall also designate a Deputy Lead who serves alongside the Documentation 
Team Lead during each term.

The Deputy Lead is responsible for:

* Assisting the Documentation Team Lead with team coordination and operations.
* Serving as a secondary point of contact for the Documentation Team.
* Participating in discussions related to the NumPy official documentation across the entire
  project.
* Receiving mentorship from the current Lead.
* Onboarding and supporting documentation contributors.
* Assuming leadership responsibilities when delegated by the Lead or when the Lead is
  temporarily unavailable.

At the conclusion of the current Lead's term, the Deputy Lead shall ordinarily succeed to 
the Documentation Team Lead role for the following one-year term, unless they decline the 
position or the team determines that an alternative leadership transition is necessary.

### Succession Planning

The Lead and Deputy Lead model is intended to:

* Promote continuity of leadership and ongoing initiatives related to the NumPy official
  documentation.
* Reduce the risk of knowledge loss during leadership transitions.
* Create opportunities for mentorship and leadership development.
* Build and sustain a diverse pipeline of future NumPy leaders.
* Ensure that leadership responsibilities are shared and distributed across the contributor
  community.

## Decision-Making

The Documentation Team operates by lazy consensus whenever possible. When consensus cannot be reached, 
decisions may be escalated to the NumPy Steering Council.

## Amendments

This charter may be amended through lazy consensus of the Documentation Team.
